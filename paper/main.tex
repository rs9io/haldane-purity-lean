\begin{abstract}
We present a Lean~4 (\id{v4.36.0}, Mathlib \id{v4.36.0}) formalization of the finite-temperature purity bootstrap for quantum spin Hamiltonians across 29 modules and 1{,}781 verified theorem constants (1{,}468 under \id{PurityBootstrap.*}) with zero \id{sorry}, zero \id{admit}, zero \id{native_decide}, and zero custom axioms---strictly within standard axioms \id{[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]}.
Our primary physical result extends the periodic spin-1 Haldane gap proof from the isotropic point ($D = 0$) to an explicit anisotropic point ($D = 1/4$) and a continuous interval around it inside the Haldane phase, while eliminating all abstract transfer-operator and signed-sector-moment hypotheses inside Lean~4's kernel:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.8em,itemsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{Anisotropic Spin-1 Haldane Gap at $(\Delta, D) = (1, 1/4)$ and Continuous Interval:} Breaking $SO(3) \to O(2)$ removes the isotropic representation-theory shortcut. Using a finite-$M$ no-collision $O(2)/SO(3)$-equivariant real symmetric transfer matrix, exact cycle contraction, first-order Trotter convergence, subsequential signed-moment convergence under a uniform Hilbert--Schmidt $\ell^2$ bound, and real $D_4 + R_z(\pi/4)$ sector projections inside Lean~4, we reduce the infinite-family gap to \emph{only} 88 finite physical thermal trace enclosures ($n=4\ldots 14$, $b \in \{31/2, 23\}$, seams $\{I, R_z(\pi), R_x(\pi), R_z(\pi/2)\}$) and a single $L=112$ real variational Rayleigh witness. From those finite premises, Lean~4 proves a simple ground energy (multiplicity~1 in \id{charpoly.roots}) and finite-volume spectral gap $\gamma_L > \frac{\log 20}{368} \approx 0.00814058$ on the concrete $3^L \times 3^L$ physical spin-1 Hamiltonian $H_{\mathrm{spin1}}(L, 1/4, I)$ for every finite even $L \ge 896$. By proving operator-monotone trace comparison and uniform purity transport in Lean~4 (\id{Spin1Interval.lean}), the \emph{exact same} center inputs further imply a simple ground energy and uniform gap $\gamma_L > \frac{\log 20}{736} \approx 0.00407029$ for every continuous anisotropy $D \in [0.249999, 0.250001]$ and every even $L \ge 1792$ (preserving $L \ge 896$ and $\gamma_L > \frac{\log 20}{368}$ on $D \in [0.2499999, 0.2500001]$). All 88 thermal enclosures are certified externally from archived 256-bit integer totals in exact rational arithmetic (worst endpoint distance $1.245 \times 10^{-6} < 10^{-5}$, 87.55\% safety margin), alongside an exact \id{int64}-residual 7-site open-chain lower bound and a 160-bit Arb enclosure of a $\chi = 32$ periodic MPS trial witness at $L = 112$.
  \item \textbf{Isotropic Spin-1 Haldane Gap at $(\Delta, D) = (1, 0)$ (OpenAI Source~1):} Applying the same concrete transfer and $D_2 \rtimes C_3$ projection bridge inside Lean~4 yields a simple ground energy and gap $\gamma_L > \frac{\log 20}{784}$ on $H_{\mathrm{spin1}}(L, 0, I)$ for every finite even $L \ge 2304$, taking only the 18 concrete physical thermal trace enclosures ($n = 4\ldots 12$, $b = 49/4$, seams $\{I, R_x(\pi)\}$) and a single $L = 72$ variational Rayleigh witness.
  \item \textbf{Zero-Oracle Formal-Verification Benchmarks (1D Interacting Ising \& 2D TFIM):} To validate our 1D symmetry-twist and 2D purity-bootstrap libraries end-to-end with zero external numerical inputs, we prove two completely self-contained perturbative benchmark theorems directly inside Lean~4's kernel: (a) a multiplet and gap theorem for the non-integrable 1D interacting Ising ring $H_\ell(g, h)$ across $(g, h) \in [\frac{15}{128}, \frac{16}{128}] \times [\frac{47}{128}, \frac{48}{128}]$ for all finite even $\ell \ge 64$; and (b) a finite-volume gap theorem ($\gamma_L > \frac{\log 2}{5} \approx 0.138629$, simple ground energy) for the concrete $2^{L^2} \times 2^{L^2}$ 2D transverse-field Ising square torus across $|J_x|, |J_y| \le 1/192$ for all finite even $L \ge 2$. While the existence of a gap in these perturbative Ising regimes is classically known, these theorems verify the full 1D twist and 2D Lie--Trotter bootstrap pipeline with zero external oracles (with the 2D benchmark additionally maintaining a data-free import closure).
\end{enumerate}
\end{abstract}

\textbf{Plain-English Summary.}
Imagine a circular necklace made of atomic magnets, where each magnet can point up, sideways, or down and prefers to point opposite to its neighbors. In 1983, physicist F.~Duncan~M.~Haldane predicted a surprising quantum effect: even as the necklace grows arbitrarily long, there is a permanent \emph{energy gap}---a fixed minimum cost to excite the ring above its lowest-energy state---so the gap never shrinks to zero.

Checking that directly by computer is impossible for large rings: a necklace of 896 spin-1 magnets has $3^{896} > 10^{427}$ possible states, far more than the number of atoms in the observable universe. Instead, the \emph{finite-temperature purity bootstrap} uses a mathematical doubling trick: tight measurements on tiny rings (up to 14 magnets) and one energy benchmark on a 112-magnet ring feed a pair of coupled inequalities that snowball from length $56 \to 112 \to 224 \to 448 \to 896$ and lock in a positive energy gap for \emph{every} larger even ring.

In this work, we formalize the entire proof machinery inside \textbf{Lean~4}, a strict mathematical proof checker that verifies every logical step from foundational axioms without trusting human algebra. Our main physics result extends the Haldane gap proof away from the high-symmetry isotropic point ($D = 0$) to an explicit anisotropic point inside the Haldane phase ($D = 1/4$, every even length $L \ge 896$) and to a continuous interval $D \in [0.249999, 0.250001]$ around it ($L \ge 1792$, with zero extra simulations), eliminating every abstract transfer-operator assumption inside Lean~4 (for both $D = 1/4$ and $D = 0$) so that only finite exact-arithmetic tiny-ring measurements and a single energy check remain outside the kernel. To stress-test our reusable 1D and 2D Lean~4 library with zero external numbers, we also include two self-contained formal-verification benchmarks in perturbative regimes where gaps are already classically known: a non-integrable 1D interacting Ising chain and a weak-coupling 2D transverse-field Ising torus.

\section{Introduction}
Proving uniform spectral gaps for infinite families of interacting quantum many-body Hamiltonians is difficult because the Hilbert-space dimension grows exponentially with system size (e.g., $3^L$ basis configurations for a spin-1 chain of length $L$, exceeding $10^{427}$ at $L = 896$).
The \emph{finite-temperature purity bootstrap}, introduced in the September~24, 2026 periodic spin-1 Haldane gap preprint~\cite{openai2026haldane} and extended to symmetry-twisted sectors and higher spatial dimensions by Grover~\cite{grover2026}, overcomes this exponential barrier by turning finite-volume thermal trace enclosures and a single variational energy witness into coupled spatial- and temporal-doubling purity contractions that hold for all sufficiently large finite even rings.

While the purity bootstrap reduces an infinite family of spectral problems to finite arithmetic checks, its end-to-end rigor rests on two delicate layers: (i) the algebraic, analytic, and representation-theoretic transfer-operator machinery linking physical Gibbs traces $\Tr(e^{-b(H_n + a n I)})$ to signed sector-moment expansions across both even and odd ring lengths, and (ii) the exact interval arithmetic and coupled bootstrap inequalities themselves.
In this work, we formalize \emph{both} layers inside Lean~4's pure kernel in the public repository \href{https://github.com/rs9io/haldane-purity-lean}{\id{rs9io/haldane-purity-lean}} (with an interactive visual companion at \url{https://rs9.com.au/haldane-gap}), apply them to certify an explicit anisotropic spin-1 Haldane point ($D = 1/4$) and continuous interval around it alongside the isotropic point ($D = 0$), and validate both the 1D symmetry-twist and 2D purity-bootstrap modules end-to-end on self-contained perturbative Ising benchmarks.

Table~\ref{tab:summary} states both the mathematical verification boundary and the physical scope of each result.
For the spin-1 theorems ($D = 1/4$, the continuous interval around $D = 1/4$, and $D = 0$), \emph{every} abstract mathematical step---including the concrete $3^L\times 3^L$ physical spin-1 Hamiltonian, explicit single-site unitary equivalence to the textbook $S^z$ weight basis (yielding many-body equivalence via tensoring), finite-$M$ transfer matrix, Trotter convergence, subsequential signed-moment convergence under a uniform $\ell^2$ bound, exact $D_4$ and $D_2$ symmetry projections, nonnegative polynomial filters (sixteen degree-14 filters for $D = 1/4$; degree-12 filters for $D = 0$), capped-mass residual bounds, temperature transfer, operator-monotone coupling transport, coupled bootstrap rounds, Rayleigh--Ritz criterion, and \id{charpoly.roots} multiplicity extraction---is proved unconditionally in Lean~4.

\section{Zero-Oracle 1D Benchmark: Interacting Ising Multiplet Theorem (Candidate~E)}
Consider the 1D interacting transverse-field Ising ring on $\ell = 2m$ qubits (Grover~\cite{grover2026}, Pauli normalization $J = 1$):
\begin{equation}
  H_\ell(g, h) \;=\; -\sum_{i=0}^{\ell-1} Z_i Z_{i+1} \;-\; g \sum_{i=0}^{\ell-1} X_i X_{i+1} \;-\; h \sum_{i=0}^{\ell-1} X_i,
  \label{eq:ising}
\end{equation}
with global spin-flip symmetry $P = \bigotimes_{i=0}^{\ell-1} X_i$ partitioning the spectrum into $+1$ and $-1$ parity sectors.

\begin{theorem}[Zero-Oracle 2D Coupling-Rectangle Multiplet Theorem]
\label{thm:candE}
In \id{candidate_e_unconditional_full_hamiltonian_theorem}, for every coupling pair $(g, h) \in [\frac{15}{128}, \frac{16}{128}] \times [\frac{47}{128}, \frac{48}{128}]$ and every finite even ring length $\ell = 2m \ge 64$, let $E_0(\ell) \le E_1(\ell) \le \dots \le E_{2^\ell - 1}(\ell)$ be all $2^\ell$ eigenvalues of $H_\ell(g, h)$ (proved equal as a multiset to \id{H_full.charpoly.roots}). Then:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.8em,itemsep=2pt]
  \item $E_0(\ell)$ is the unique ground energy of the $+1$ parity sector ($P = +1$) and $E_1(\ell)$ is the unique ground energy of the $-1$ parity sector ($P = -1$);
  \item the low-energy doublet is exponentially narrow:
  \[
    0 \;\le\; E_1(\ell) - E_0(\ell) \;\le\; \frac{1}{20} \exp\!\left(-\frac{\log 20}{128}\,\ell\right);
  \]
  \item every higher excitation is uniformly gapped above the doublet:
  \[
    E_i(\ell) - E_1(\ell) \;>\; \frac{\log 8}{48} \;\approx\; 0.0433217 \qquad \forall\, 2 \le i < 2^\ell.
  \]
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

Three ingredients make this 1D benchmark completely self-contained inside Lean~4:
(i) \id{LieTrotter.lean} and \id{IsingTrotter.lean} establish exact 3-way Lie--Trotter convergence and twisted checkerboard positive-semidefinite Gram transfer-matrix contractions for both untwisted and $P$-twisted partition functions, eliminating all abstract transfer-matrix hypotheses;
(ii) \id{BlockGershgorin.lean} and \id{CandidateE/Corner*.lean} execute exact rational block-Gershgorin upper enclosures at all four corners of the coupling rectangle at $\ell = 8$, paired with an exact integer $\chi = 2$ periodic MPS Rayleigh witness at $\ell = 16$ (\id{IsingMPS.lean}) and log-convexity of sector traces across the rectangle (\id{MatrixTools.lean}, \id{CandidateE/RectangleTheorem.lean}), establishing $Q_+(8, 6) \ge 877/1000$ uniformly across the rectangle;
(iii) three exact upward-rounded 1D coupled purity-bootstrap rounds advance $(8, 6) \to (64, 48)$ and extract the doublet and gap bounds via \id{SymmetryTwists.lean}.

\section{Eliminating All Abstract Transfer Hypotheses for Spin-1 Chains}
For spin-1 chains, the purity bootstrap requires both an even-length \id{SpectralSystem1D} representation and \emph{signed} sector-moment expansions holding across both even and odd ring lengths $n \ge 4$.
Rather than postulating an infinite-dimensional compact transfer operator on $L^2$-path space, we build a completely elementary, finite-dimensional, kernel-verified transfer limit in five modules: \id{Spin1Hamiltonian.lean}, \id{Spin1Compactness.lean}, \id{Spin1Transfer.lean}, \id{Spin1PhysicalGap.lean}, and \id{Spin1Interval.lean}.

\subsection{Concrete Physical Spin-1 Hamiltonian and Weight-Basis Unitary Equivalence}
In \id{Spin1Hamiltonian.lean}, we work in the real Cartesian spin-1 basis $\R^3$ with skew-symmetric generators $(G_\alpha)_{ij} = \varepsilon_{\alpha ij}$ ($\alpha \in \{0,1,2\}$) and diagonal anisotropy matrix $\mathrm{SzSq\_mat} = \diag(1, 1, 0)$.
We construct an explicit $3 \times 3$ complex matrix $U_{\mathrm{cart}\to sz}$ and prove in Lean~4:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.6em,itemsep=2pt]
  \item \id{U_cart_to_sz_unitary}: $U_{\mathrm{cart}\to sz} U_{\mathrm{cart}\to sz}^\dagger = U_{\mathrm{cart}\to sz}^\dagger U_{\mathrm{cart}\to sz} = I_3$;
  \item \id{U_cart_to_sz_conj_S}: $U_{\mathrm{cart}\to sz}(-i G_\alpha)U_{\mathrm{cart}\to sz}^\dagger = S^\alpha$ for all $\alpha \in \{x, y, z\}$ in the standard weight basis $(+1, 0, -1)$;
  \item \id{U_cart_to_sz_conj_SzSq}: $U_{\mathrm{cart}\to sz}\,\mathrm{SzSq\_mat}\,U_{\mathrm{cart}\to sz}^\dagger = (S^z)^2 = \diag(1, 0, 1)$.
\end{itemize}
Because $(-i)^2 = -1$, each Heisenberg bond $\sum_\alpha S^\alpha_j \otimes (g S^\alpha g^\mathsf{T})_k$ equals $-\sum_\alpha (G_\alpha)_j \otimes (g G_\alpha g^\mathsf{T})_k$ in the real Cartesian basis, and tensoring $U_{\mathrm{cart}\to sz}$ across all $n$ sites yields the many-body unitary equivalence to the textbook weight-basis ring Hamiltonian.
For any $n \ge 3$, anisotropy $D \in \R$, and $3 \times 3$ real matrix $g$, the concrete $3^n \times 3^n$ real symmetric Hamiltonian is
\begin{equation}
  H_{\mathrm{spin1}}(n, D, g) \;=\; \sum_{j=0}^{n-2} h_{j, j+1}(I) \;+\; h_{n-1, 0}(g) \;+\; D \sum_{j=0}^{n-1} (\mathrm{SzSq\_mat})_j,
  \label{eq:Hspin1}
\end{equation}
where $g$ rotates only site~$0$ of the wrap-around bond $(n-1, 0)$.
The concrete physical partition function with energy shift $a \in \R$ at inverse temperature $b > 0$ is
\[
  Z_{\mathrm{spin1}}(n, D, a, g, b) \;=\; \Tr\!\Big(\exp\big(-b\,(H_{\mathrm{spin1}}(n, D, g) + a n I)\big)\Big),
\]
proved equal to $\sum_{k \in (\mathrm{Fin}\,3)^n} e^{-b(E_k + a n)}$ (\id{Z_spin1_eq_sum_exp}).

\subsection{Finite-$M$ No-Collision Equivariant Transfer Matrix and Trotter Limit}
Fix $M \ge 1$ and $\tau = b / M$. On the finite configuration space $\mathrm{Word}(M) = (\mathrm{Fin}\,4)^M$ (where index $0$ represents an unoccupied bond slice and indices $1, 2, 3$ carry the $\R^3$ vector label $\alpha$), define the single-slice $3 \times 3$ matrix kernel
\[
  \Phi_\tau(u, v) \;=\; \begin{cases}
    I_3 & \text{if } u = 0, v = 0, \\
    \sqrt{\tau}\, G_{v-1} & \text{if } u = 0, v \in \{1,2,3\}, \\
    \sqrt{\tau}\, G_{u-1} & \text{if } u \in \{1,2,3\}, v = 0, \\
    0 & \text{if } u, v \in \{1,2,3\},
  \end{cases}
\]
and the $4^M \times 4^M$ real matrix $K_M(D, a, b)$ with entries
\[
  (K_M)_{w, w'} \;=\; \Tr_3\!\left(\prod_{t=0}^{M-1} \Big(e^{-\tau V(D, a)} \, \Phi_\tau(w_t, w'_t)\Big)\right), \qquad V(D, a) = D\,\mathrm{SzSq\_mat} + a I_3.
\]
We define the predicate \id{IsSpin1Rot g} requiring $g$ to be orthogonal and covariant with the generators ($g G_\alpha g^\mathsf{T} = \sum_\beta g_{\beta\alpha} G_\beta$), and \id{IsAxialRot g} additionally requiring preservation of $\mathrm{SzSq\_mat}$. This zero-collision construction enjoys four exact properties proved in \id{Spin1Transfer.lean}:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.8em,itemsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{Exact real symmetry:} $K_M^\mathsf{T} = K_M$ (\id{K_mat_transpose}), because $\Phi_\tau(v, u) = \Phi_\tau(u, v)$ preserves the time-ordered product.
  \item \textbf{Exact $O(2)/SO(3)$-equivariance:} With $U_M(g) = (1 \oplus g)^{\otimes M}$ on $\mathrm{Word}(M)$, we prove $U_M(g) K_M = K_M U_M(g)$ (\id{K_mat_comm_U}) for any $g$ satisfying \id{IsSpin1Rot g} and preserving the onsite exponential $e^{-\tau V(D, a)}$---in particular, every axial rotation $g$ satisfying \id{IsAxialRot g} (including $R_z(\pi/4)$, which pairs real irreducible sectors) at arbitrary $D$, and the cyclic axis permutation $C_3$ at $D = 0$.
  \item \textbf{Exact cycle contraction and $O(1/M)$ Trotter limit:} For every $n \ge 3$ (even or odd), $M \ge 0$, and $g$ satisfying \id{IsSpin1Rot g}, pure finite-sum expansion gives $\Tr(K_M^n U_M(g)) = \Tr(T_{\mathrm{slice}}(M)^M)$ (\id{trace_K_pow_mul_U_eq_trace_T_slice_pow}), where $T_{\mathrm{slice}}(M) = I - \frac{b}{M}(H_{\mathrm{spin1}} + a n I) + O(1/M^2)$ in operator norm. By \id{tendsto_trace_pow_of_first_order} (\id{Spin1Compactness.lean}), for every $g$ satisfying \id{IsSpin1Rot g},
  \[
    \lim_{M \to \infty} \Tr(K_M^n U_M(g)) \;=\; Z_{\mathrm{spin1}}(n, D, a, g, b) \qquad (\forall\, n \ge 3,\; b > 0).
  \]
  \item \textbf{Uniform Hilbert--Schmidt bound:} Counting the $1 + 6 = 7$ nonzero single-slice label pairs and using $\|G_\alpha\|_\infty = 1$ yields the $M$-independent bound (\id{K_mat_hilbert_schmidt_le})
  \[
    \Tr(K_M^2) \;\le\; 9 \exp\!\big(2 b (|D| + |a| + 3)\big) \;=:\; C(D, a, b).
  \]
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Subsequential Signed-Moment Convergence and Real Symmetric Sector Projections}
In \id{Spin1Compactness.lean}, \id{l2_seq_compact_moments} takes any family of finite real eigenvalue lists $\{f_{M, s, i}\}$ uniformly bounded in $\ell^2$ ($\sum_i f_{M,s,i}^2 \le C$), sorts each list by decreasing $|f_{M,s,i}|$ while preserving eigenvalue signs, pads with zeros, and applies Tychonoff sequential compactness on $[-\sqrt{C}, \sqrt{C}]^{\mathrm{Fin}\,k \times \N}$.
The decreasing rearrangement gives $|f_{M,s,i}| \le \sqrt{C/(i+1)}$, hence the uniform tail estimate
\[
  \sum_{i \ge N} |f_{M,s,i}|^n \;\le\; C \left(\frac{C}{N+1}\right)^{(n-2)/2} \xrightarrow[N \to \infty]{} 0 \qquad (\forall\, n \ge 3),
\]
establishing subsequential convergence of all signed $n$-th moments ($n \ge 3$, even and odd) with absolutely summable $n$-th powers for the limit sequences.

\paragraph{Operator-monotone purity transport.}
Because $H_{\mathrm{spin1}}(n, D, I) - H_{\mathrm{spin1}}(n, D_*, I) = (D - D_*) V_n$ where $V_n = \sum_{j=0}^{n-1} (\mathrm{SzSq\_mat})_j$ is diagonal with $0 \preceq V_n \preceq n I$, we prove an elementary 15-line operator-monotone thermal trace bound (\id{matZ_anti_of_le}: $A \preceq B \implies \Tr(e^{-b B}) \le \Tr(e^{-b A})$ for $b \ge 0$, directly from eigenbasis Jensen and basis completeness).
Taking both signs of $D - D_*$ with $|D - D_*| \le h$ cancels per-site energy shifts and yields the uniform purity transport inequalities (\id{spin1_S_transport}, \id{spin1_T_transport}):
\[
  S(n, b; D) \;\ge\; e^{-2 n b h}\, S(n, b; D_*), \qquad T(m, b; D) \;\ge\; e^{-2 m b h}\, T(m, b; D_*).
\]
Transporting the center defect upper bounds $p_* = 0.1215302$ and $q_* = 0.1203214$ at $(n, b) = (56, 23)$ via $1 - x \le e^{-x}$ to radius $h = 10^{-6}$ gives initial defect bounds at most $0.1237932$ and $0.1248536$, which contract in five exact rational doubling rounds (\id{decide +kernel}) to at most $0.0016762$ and $0.0001751$ (both $< 1/100$) at $(1792, 736)$; restarting the $D$-parametric bootstrap on \id{spin1SpectralSystem} at $(D, a)$ immediately yields Theorem~\ref{thm:candA_interval}(1) (and four rounds at $h = 10^{-7}$ yield defect bounds at most $0.0061353$ and $0.0011355$, both $< 1/100$, at $(896, 368)$).

\section{Zero-Oracle 2D Benchmark: Transverse-Field Ising Square Torus}
To validate our higher-dimensional purity-bootstrap module (\id{HigherDim.lean}, generalizing Grover~\cite{grover2026}, Sec.~II to side-length cutoff $L \ge 2$) end-to-end on a concrete 2D quantum Hamiltonian with zero external numerical inputs, we add three self-contained modules (\id{Ising2DTrotter.lean}, \id{Ising2DSeed.lean}, \id{Ising2DGap.lean}) whose import closure is entirely free of 1D certificate tables.

\[
  H_{\mathrm{2d}}(L, L, J_x, J_y) \;=\; -\sum_{v} X_v \;-\; \sum_{v} \bigl(J_x Z_v Z_{v+\hat{x}} + J_y Z_v Z_{v+\hat{y}}\bigr).
\]
In \id{Ising2DTrotter.lean}, we express $Z_a(L_x, L_y, b) = e^{-b a L_x L_y} \Tr(e^{-b H_{\mathrm{2d}}})$ as the $M \to \infty$ limit of a single 3D classical Ising sum $Z_M$ (strictly positive history weights for $b > 0, M \ge 1$) admitting the temporal Lie--Trotter trace $\Tr[(DK)^M]$ and two exact real symmetric spatial transfer cuts $\Tr(T_x^{L_x})$ and $\Tr(T_y^{L_y})$.

Passing finite-$M$ spatial Gram inequalities to $M \to \infty$ yields \id{ising_2d_unconditional_rectangle_charpoly_gap}: for every $|J_x|, |J_y| \le 1/192$ and every finite even $L \ge 2$, the roots multiset of $H_{\mathrm{2d}}(L, L, J_x, J_y)$ decomposes as $E_0 ::_m \mathrm{rest}$ with $|\mathrm{rest}| + 1 = 2^{L^2}$ and $E - E_0 > \frac{\log 2}{5} \approx 0.138629$ for all $E \in \mathrm{rest}$.

\section{Related Work and Outlook}
Our formalization builds directly on the finite-temperature purity bootstrap of~\cite{openai2026haldane} and the symmetry-twist and higher-dimensional extensions of Grover~\cite{grover2026}.
Independently, Lu~\cite{lu2026} formalized a finite-Gram/Trotter unconditional Lean~4 theorem for a mixed-field Ising chain and reported improved finite-input isotropic spin-1 gap bounds ($\gamma_L > 0.0729$ for even $L \ge 36$) together with a $D = 4$ single-ion anisotropy example.
Earlier analytic stability of ground-state degeneracy and exponentially small splitting under weak symmetric perturbations was proved by Lapa and Levin~\cite{lapalevin2021}, while Tasaki~\cite{tasaki2026}---using the analytic machinery and numerical inputs of~\cite{openai2026haldane}---proved uniqueness among translation-invariant infinite-chain ground states for sufficiently small $|D|$, showing that this state is gapped and continuous in $D$, without an explicit finite-$D$ certificate at $D = 1/4$.

Two natural next steps follow from the verified infrastructure in \id{haldane-purity-lean}: (i) widening the certified continuous interval around $D = 1/4$ and extending the pipeline deeper into the non-perturbative Haldane phase toward $D = 0.50$ and the Haldane-to-large-$D$ transition ($D_c \approx 0.968$); and (ii) investigating non-perturbative 2D quantum spin models (such as frustrated spin bilayers and honeycomb AKLT models) on top of our verified 2D purity-bootstrap modules.
